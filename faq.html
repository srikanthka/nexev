Products & Specifications 6 questions

What is a BMS and do I need one for my battery pack?

A Battery Management System (BMS) protects your Li-Ion cells from overcharge, overdischarge, overcurrent, and short circuit. It is essential for any lithium battery pack — operating a Li-Ion pack without a BMS is a safety risk. NexEV offers 1S 3A, 2S 8A, and 3S 10A BMS boards suitable for small EV and backup power applications.

Which kit should I choose — Kit A, Kit B, or Kit C?

Kit A (1S, ₹249) is for single-cell 3.7V applications like sensors and small devices. Kit B (2S, ₹349) builds a 7.4V pack suitable for small motors and power tools. Kit C (3S, ₹549) produces 11.1V/12V — ideal for e-bike lights, backup power, and small robotics. Each kit includes BMS, charger, nickel strip, wire, and insulation.

Are the products compatible with standard 18650 cells?

Yes. All NexEV components — nickel strips, cell holders, BMS boards, and chargers — are designed for standard 18650 cylindrical lithium cells.

What is the HSN code for NexEV products?

Nickel strip: HSN 7506. Silicone wire: HSN 8544. Kapton tape: HSN 3919. Insulation paper: HSN 4802. BMS boards: HSN 8542. Connectors: HSN 8536. Chargers: HSN 8504.

Do the products come with warranty?

Products are covered against manufacturing defects. Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery for damaged or defective items after inspection. See our Return Policy for full details.

What is the difference between the nickel strip and insulation barley paper?

Nickel strip is the conductor — it connects cells in series or parallel via spot welding. Insulation barley paper sits between cells and on top of cell terminals to prevent short circuits. Both are needed for a safe battery pack build.
