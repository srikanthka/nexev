About NexEV

Driven by Electric Future

Karnataka-based manufacturer and distributor of e-scooter, e-bike battery packs, BMS boards, and EV components for India.

Est. 2026 · Karnataka GSTIN: 29AALCN2942C1ZF
Our Mission

Making EV components accessible to every Indian workshop and builder

NexEV was founded in Bengaluru, Karnataka in 2026 with a clear purpose: to bring quality, affordable EV battery components within reach of Indian workshops, dealers, and DIY builders. India's electric vehicle revolution is accelerating — but the component supply chain hasn't kept up.

Workshops struggle to source reliable battery management systems, nickel strips, and assembly kits locally. NexEV exists to close that gap.

What We Do

End-to-end EV component supply

E-Scooter & E-Bike Components

Battery packs, BMS boards, connectors, and wiring for light electric two-wheelers. Built for Indian road conditions and voltage standards.

DIY Battery Assembly Kits

Complete kit solutions — Kit A (1S), Kit B (2S), Kit C (3S) — with everything needed to build a Li-Ion battery pack from scratch. Used by hobbyists, repair shops, and small manufacturers.

Wholesale & Distribution

Bulk pricing tiers for workshops, dealers, and distributors. No minimum on retail orders. GST invoices included. Pan-India shipping via Speed Post and Delhivery.
