Privacy Policy — Nexev BMS

Last updated: 17-08-2026 · Applies to the Nexev BMS mobile application (com.nexev.diveebms)

The short version. Nexev BMS talks to your battery over Bluetooth and keeps what it learns on your phone.

There is no account and no sign-up. We do not run analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting. We do not sell or share personal data. In normal rider use, nothing you do in the app is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

This app is published by Nexev Private Limited ("we", "us"), of 2F, No. 15 , Anjandri Layout, 1st Main Cross , Kodigehalli Main Road, Kodigehalli, Kadugodi, Bengaluru, Karnataka-560067. For anything in this policy, contact privacy@nexev.in.

2. What we collect

We collect nothing on our servers from ordinary use of this app. The app has no user accounts, no login, and no telemetry back to us. It contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.

Everything described below is stored on your device unless a section says otherwise.

3. Information stored on your device

WhatWhy
Identifier and name of the last battery you connected to To offer one-tap reconnection
Readings from your battery — charge, voltage, current, temperature, per-cell voltages, faults, event log To display them, chart trends and let you export them

4. Permissions, and exactly what each is for

Bluetooth

Required. The app uses Bluetooth Low Energy to find and connect to your battery management system. It communicates only with the battery you choose.

Location

On Android, the operating system requires location permission before an app may scan for Bluetooth devices at all. This is an Android platform rule, not a choice we made. We do not collect, store or transmit your location for this purpose, and the app does not read your position when scanning.

Optional, fleet use only. If your operator enables geofencing for a rental battery, the app compares your position against a boundary the operator sets, so it can tell you when a battery leaves its permitted area. This applies only to fleet-managed batteries. It is not used in personal use.

Camera

Optional. Used only when you open the QR scanner to identify or provision a battery. Images are processed on the device to read the code and are not stored, uploaded or retained.

Notifications

Optional. Used to alert you when your battery crosses a limit you have configured — for example a temperature or charge threshold. Notifications are generated on your device from readings taken from your battery. Nothing is sent through a push server.

5. Battery data

A battery management system records its own history: power-ups, protection faults, thermal warnings, configuration changes and charge milestones. When you read that log, it is transferred from the battery to your phone over Bluetooth and shown to you.

This is technical data about a piece of equipment. It becomes personal data only where the equipment is linked to you — for example a rental battery assigned to your account by an operator.

6. Fleet and operator features

The app includes optional features for organisations that operate batteries as a fleet. These are not part of ordinary personal use and are not reachable in the app's default configuration.

Where an operator has configured a message broker, the app can send battery telemetry, rental session start and end events, and geofence alerts to that operator's own server. If no broker is configured, which is the default, no such data is transmitted and these functions do nothing.

In that arrangement the operator, not us, decides what is collected and for how long it is kept, and is the data controller for it. Ask your operator for their privacy notice.

7. Exporting and sharing

You can export the battery event log, charge history and health history as CSV files. Exporting is always something you start. The file is created on your device and handed to the share sheet you choose — email, messaging, cloud storage or another app. Once you send it somewhere, that destination's privacy policy governs it.

8. Third-party components

The app is built with open-source libraries for Bluetooth, charting, maps, QR scanning, local notifications and local storage. These run on your device. None of them is an analytics, advertising or tracking service.

One exception is worth naming: if you open the swap-station map in fleet mode, map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap, which will see your IP address as any website would. The map is not shown in ordinary personal use.

9. Children

This is a technical tool for battery equipment. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

10. Keeping and deleting your data

Data stays on your device until you remove it. You can:

  • reset the odometer and stored counters from Configuration;
  • clear app storage from your device's system settings; or
  • uninstall the app, which removes everything it stored.

Because we hold no copy of it, we cannot delete it for you — and equally, we have nothing about you to disclose, sell or lose.

11. Security

App data is held in storage private to the app. PINs and recovery phrases are stored only as one-way hashes. Communication with your battery is a direct Bluetooth link between your phone and the battery.

No system is perfectly secure. A device that is rooted, jailbroken or shared with someone else weakens these protections, as it would for any app.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to certain processing — for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, or Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act.

Because this app stores its data on your device and we hold no copy, you can exercise most of these rights directly and immediately, as described in section 10. If you believe we hold personal data about you and want to make a request, write to privacy@nexev.in. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.
